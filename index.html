Your first flight.

Find parked aircraft at Meadows Airport in the northeast. Press E near a plane or helicopter to borrow it. Use the airport compass button beside Dispatch for directions.

Planes

Helicopters

Default plane W lowers the nose; S raises it. Hold Shift / Space to adjust throttle, which holds when released. Plane exit is X after landing. F deploys a parachute; R recovers. Use Settings to choose presets or change these keys.

Flight instruments show speed, height above ground (AGL), and throttle. Avoid low airspeed in planes; add throttle and lower the nose if a stall warning appears. Approach the runway straight, ease the throttle, and descend gently. For helicopters, hold Shift to settle into a hover before landing.

Aircraft weapons: Hold left mouse for a machine gun in the fighter, police helicopter or heavy lift helicopter. Hold right mouse in the fighter to lock an airborne aircraft or tank near the crosshair for one second, then launch one of six guided missiles. Armed helicopters instead carry 24 straight-flying rockets on right mouse. Keep helicopter aim ahead of the cabin. Land and stop at an airfield without firing for three seconds to rearm.

Passenger and cargo jobs require a helicopter. Land on the marked pad and stop for two seconds. Tours and races use elevated rings; match the altitude shown on your compass. News helicopters investigate incidents; police aircraft search around the last place they saw you.

Borrowed armour.

Approach a stopped, unoccupied tank and press E to borrow it. Drive with WASD; move the mouse to aim the turret independently. Hold LEFT MOUSE to fire the cannon. The reticle and cannon panel show when the 4.5-second reload finishes. Press R to recover a stuck tank, or stop and press E to hop out.

After 60 continuous seconds at four stars, Northwatch can send heavy armour whether you are on foot, driving or flying. Tank cannons target ground vehicles only. Attacking the base also authorises armour. An eight-second warning announces dispatch; the tank status follows its approach, search and return. Tanks use broad roads on the outskirts.

Northwatch defenders.

Soldiers patrol the base. An attack on Northwatch, or four-star heat, calls a four-soldier squad in a military truck after a six-second warning. The carrier follows roads and stops before soldiers step out into clear spaces. Soldiers aim and fire short bursts at ground targets, use the last place they saw you, and regroup when heat clears. Buildings, fences, terrain and vehicles provide cover. Toys and moving vehicles can knock soldiers down. Watch the infantry status and amber markers on the minimap.

A stopped carrier can be borrowed with E and driven with WASD. Its crew exits when safe. The carrier has 240 hull health; repairs keep that capacity, and it is too large for civilian garage slots.

Contracts earn pocket money and style points. Heat rises only if police see or hear trouble, or a resident calls for help. Help calls take two seconds; disappear from patrols to cool off. The fourth star brings Northwatch into the chase: a jet intercepts aircraft, while a military helicopter pursues road vehicles. Watch the scramble warning beside your heat stars and the amber diamond on your minimap; break sight to cool off. Follow the compass and minimap to Sprinkle Garage for toys, supplies, saved rides, paint, repairs, and upgrades.
